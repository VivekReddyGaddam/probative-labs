# Independence

Probative Labs checks what AI vendors claim. That only works if nobody can pay to change the answer. These rules are public so customers, vendors and auditors can hold Probative Labs to them.

1. **Probative Labs sells no AI.** It does not build, resell or implement AI products, and takes no referral or partner fees from AI vendors.
2. **Vendors cannot pay for results.** Probative Labs does not accept payment from an AI vendor to evaluate, certify or rank its product. A vendor cannot buy, preview, edit or suppress a customer's results or a benchmark figure.
3. **Customers pay per measured program, never a share of the savings found.** A share of savings would reward finding value, which is the opposite of neutral measurement.
4. **The method is open.** The method and engine are open source under Apache-2.0. Every result records the engine version, data fingerprints and settings, so anyone with the same data can rerun it and get the same answer. Method changes are published with their validation before they ship.
5. **Results belong to the customer.** Probative Labs never publishes a customer's results, or names a customer, without written permission.
6. **Benchmarks are anonymous and aggregate.** Contributions contain no company, team or person names, no ticket text and no money amounts. A benchmark figure is shown only when at least five organizations have contributed for the same product and measure. Contributions are never sold.
7. **Conflicts are disclosed.** If anyone working on a customer's analysis has a financial interest in an AI vendor being measured, the customer is told before the work starts.

If these rules change, the change will be recorded here, with its date, before it takes effect. Raise concerns in [GitHub issues](https://github.com/VivekReddyGaddam/probative-labs/issues).

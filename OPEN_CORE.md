# What is open source and what is Probative Labs Cloud

Probative Labs is an independent check on what AI vendors claim. A checker that asks for blind trust is no better than the claims it checks, so the method, and the engine that implements it, are open: any data scientist, auditor or skeptical CFO can read the code, rerun an analysis and get the same answer.

The open-source engine shows you, privately, whether your AI works. Probative Labs Cloud proves it to the people who act on it, from your CFO to the vendor, and turns what it finds into money back and a better deal at renewal, every month:

- **Proof for the renewal.** I run the analysis, independently of the vendor, and the question is locked with a server timestamp before any result exists, so nobody can move the goalposts. The vendor can rerun every number with the open engine, but can't pay to change it. Once five organizations contribute, you also see your result beside your peers'.
- **An early warning when the AI slips.** Read-only connectors pull fresh tickets, and the checks run by themselves every month. An alert reaches Slack, Google Chat or any webhook when claimed resolutions stop holding, a bill has items to dispute, or the data goes stale, so a vendor update nobody announced shows up at the next check, not at the renewal.
- **Money back on your AI bill.** Per resolution, per conversation, per session, in assists or credits, or per seat: send each bill or usage export to the program's upload address and it is checked against your own records as it arrives. Billing errors become a dispute pack the vendor can recheck; usage shows units spent in test instances and on conversations that failed. Every model ends in what each resolution that held cost, and I help you take the findings to the vendor.
- **Answers for your CFO and board.** A portfolio view across every AI program and a cost ledger, with the unit values that turn hours into money signed off by finance, not by the vendor. Every registration, sign-off, result and download is kept in an append-only audit log.
- **Nothing for your team to run.** Read-only connectors (in beta) pull tickets, and any system can send the AI's conversations and bills to the program's upload address. The checks run every month by themselves, so after setup nobody on your team has to run anything. In early access, I set up each program with you and present the first findings to IT, finance and procurement.

## The split

**Every analysis is in both:** evidence pack, resolution audit, bill reconciliation and dispute pack, regression watch, action audit, vendor bake-off, measurement plan, benchmark contributions and re-performance. The method and the numbers are the same everywhere.

## Principles

1. **Every number Probative Labs Cloud shows can be reproduced with the open engine.** Cloud results record the engine version, data hashes and configuration, and Cloud never runs a private fork of the method.
2. **Method improvements land in open source first.** That includes new designs, diagnostics, audits and grade definitions. Cloud competes on independence, automation, benchmarks and service, not on hidden statistics.
3. **No feature removal.** Something released as open source stays open source.
4. **Independence.** Pricing is per measured program, never a share of the savings found, and AI vendors cannot pay for results. See [INDEPENDENCE.md](INDEPENDENCE.md).

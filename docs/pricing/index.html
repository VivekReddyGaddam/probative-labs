Questions

What does a pilot need from us?
A ticket export or read-only access to your service desk (ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, Zendesk, Freshservice, BMC Helix or Ivanti), the dates each team got the AI, your AI costs, and a finance partner to agree the loaded hourly cost. No production changes and no access for writing.
How soon do we see evidence?
If the AI is already live, usually within weeks of getting the data, using your real rollout history. For new rollouts we design the waves first, so the evidence is stronger.
What if the AI is not working?
Then the evidence pack says so, with the reason and what to fix. "Fix" and "stop" are standard outcomes, and one right-sizing decision often covers the subscription.
Can it run inside our cloud?
Yes. Enterprise includes single-tenant or customer-hosted deployment on your AWS, Azure or Google Cloud account.
Can we use the open-source engine on our own?
Yes, under Apache 2.0. Customers pay for connectors, the hosted portfolio, governance features and our methodologists' time.

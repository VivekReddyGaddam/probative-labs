Questions

Where should we start?
If you pay per resolution, or simply want a fast answer, start with a resolution audit: two weeks from your exports to a readout, and it works even if every team got the AI at once. If teams got the AI on different dates, or a rollout is coming, add a full program verification.
Do AI vendors pay you?
No. We take no fees from AI vendors, and they cannot pay for, preview or suppress results. See our independence rules.
What does a pilot need from us?
A ticket export or read-only access to your service desk (ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, Zendesk, Freshservice, BMC Helix or Ivanti). For an audit, an export of the AI's conversations. For a full verification, the dates each team got the AI, your AI costs, and a finance partner to agree the loaded hourly cost. No production changes and no access for writing.
How soon do we see evidence?
If the AI is already live, usually within weeks of getting the data, using your real rollout history. For new rollouts we design the waves first, so the evidence is stronger.
What if the AI is not working?
Then the evidence pack says so, with the reason and what to fix. "Fix" and "stop" are standard outcomes, and one right-sizing decision often covers the subscription.
Can it run inside our cloud?
Yes. Portfolio includes single-tenant or customer-hosted deployment on your AWS, Azure or Google Cloud account.
Can we use the open-source engine on our own?
Yes, under Apache 2.0, including the resolution audit. Customers pay for continuous monthly verification, connectors, peer benchmarks, governance features and our methodologists' time.

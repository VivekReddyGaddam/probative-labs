What you get by having us run it

Five things the free engine can't give you on its own.

An independent finding

Run the engine yourself and a vendor can dismiss the result as your own numbers. With Cloud, our team runs the analysis, the question is locked with a timestamp before any result exists, and anyone with the data, the vendor included, can rerun every number with the open engine.

It runs by itself

Read-only connectors pull fresh tickets, and any system can send conversations and bills to the program's upload address. The checks run every month, and an alert reaches Slack, Google Chat or any webhook when the AI gets worse, a bill has items to dispute, or data goes stale.

Peers to compare with

See your result beside the median and middle range for the same AI product. Contributions are anonymous, and a figure appears once five organizations contribute: nobody can build this alone.

A record auditors trust

Who locked the question, who signed off the unit values, every result and every download, kept in an append-only audit log. Finance owns the numbers that turn hours into money.

People who do this every day

A methodologist sets up each program with you, presents the findings to finance and procurement, and helps you take a dispute pack to the vendor.

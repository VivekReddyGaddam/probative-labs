How it works

A resolution holds only if the person does not need help again.

  1. Find returns

    For each claimed resolution, look for a ticket from the same person within a week, about the same type of issue.

  2. Find the normal rate

    People come back after your agents' resolutions too. We measure that in the same months, for the same issue types.

  3. Count only the excess

    Only returns above the normal rate count against the AI, so unrelated tickets do not inflate the result.

  4. State the uncertainty

    A range from resampling people, robustness checks, and a high, medium or low confidence with the reasons.

  5. Put a price on it

    If you pay per resolution, the audit shows how much went to resolutions that did not hold.

Did not hold = (return rate after the AI − return rate after people) ÷ (1 − return rate after people)

What we need

Two exports. No production access.

  • The AI's conversations or automated resolutions: who, when, outcome and topic
  • Your ticket export for the same dates plus a week (ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, Zendesk, Freshservice, BMC Helix, Ivanti or any CSV)

People are pseudonymized as the files are read. Results describe the AI, never individual employees or customers.

Questions

Do we need a staggered rollout?
No. The audit compares the AI's resolutions with your agents' resolutions in the same months, so it works even if every team got the AI on the same day.
What if the AI's resolutions hold up?
Then the audit says so: the range includes zero, and the report states that the AI's resolutions are not shown to fail more often than your agents'.
What can it miss?
Someone who gives up on the bot and phones or asks a colleague leaves no ticket, so the audit is a floor unless every channel creates a ticket. It also needs the same person identifier in both systems; the report shows how many people it could match.
Can we run it ourselves?
Yes. The audit, the bill reconciliation and the watch are part of the open-source engine: probative audit, probative reconcile, probative watch. Customers pay for monthly checks on live data, connectors, benchmarks and our readout.

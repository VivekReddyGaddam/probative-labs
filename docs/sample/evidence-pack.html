Method appendix

Estimator

Effects are estimated with the imputation estimator for staggered adoption (Borusyak, Jaravel and Spiess, Review of Economic Studies, 2024). Team and week effects are fitted by weighted least squares using only team-weeks without the AI (comparison teams, and AI teams before their rollout). For every team-week with the AI, the fitted model predicts the outcome it would have had without it; the effect is the observed minus the predicted outcome. Effects are averaged with weights equal to the number of tickets in each team-week, so the headline is an effect per ticket.

Because AI team-weeks are only ever compared with untreated data, the estimator avoids the bias of ordinary two-way fixed-effects regressions under staggered rollouts with effects that change over time. A single rollout date with a comparison group reduces to two-group difference-in-differences.

  • Design: Staggered rollout (imputation difference-in-differences)
  • Teams analysed: 40 (30 with the AI, 10 without it in the window; 3 rollout wave(s))
  • Window: 2025-01-06 to 2026-07-05 (78 weeks)
  • Rollout weeks that start mid-week are dropped for the team concerned (partly exposed).

Uncertainty

Ranges come from a bootstrap that resamples whole teams (499 draws, seed 20261014), stratified by rollout wave so every draw keeps the design. Intervals are percentile intervals at the 95% level. Tickets within a team are not independent, so resampling teams, not tickets, gives honest uncertainty.

Work typing and mix adjustment

Each ticket is typed from its category fields (and any custom rules). Types below the configured share of baseline volume are folded into 'Other'. Mix-adjusted outcomes subtract each type's baseline mean (computed on untreated team-weeks only) before averaging, so the engine compares tickets only with tickets of the same type. The headline (unadjusted) estimate is always reported beside it.

The descriptive before/after table reweights each period's per-type means to the AI teams' before-rollout mix; it has no comparison group and is not causal.

Diagnostics

  • Pre-trend: lead coefficients for the weeks before rollout are estimated on untreated team-weeks with team and week effects; their joint Wald test uses the bootstrap covariance. Fail if p < 0.05.
  • Placebo dates: data after each team's real rollout is removed and the rollout is moved earlier; fail if the placebo range excludes zero.
  • Placebo outcomes: metrics the AI cannot affect (e.g. share of tickets opened after hours) are estimated with the same design; fail if the range excludes zero.
  • Leave-one-team-out: the estimate is recomputed dropping each AI team; fail if one team flips the sign of a significant effect or moves it by more than half.
  • Category drift: Jensen-Shannon distance of the category mix before and after rollout, AI teams versus comparison teams; fail if categories appear or vanish.
  • Power: minimum detectable effect = 2.80 × standard error (two-sided 5% test, 80% power).

Quality guardrails

Each guardrail is estimated with the same design (type-adjusted). Pass: the upper end of the harm range is below the threshold. Breach: the harm exceeds the threshold and its range excludes zero. Watch: otherwise. Any breach withholds the program's value. The false deflection rate is judged on its level (Wilson interval) against its threshold.

Value and ROI

Capacity value = (agent hours saved + verified deflections × baseline hours for that type − false deflections × extra hours) × loaded hourly cost. Cash value = capacity value × realization share + documented realized savings. ROI = (value − total cost) ÷ total cost.

Hours saved enter through their team-bootstrap draws; each customer assumption enters as a triangular distribution (low, most likely, high). The simulation reports the 10th, 50th and 90th percentiles. Payback = one-time costs ÷ (monthly value − monthly recurring cost).

Deflection counts only when the evidence grade counts, no guardrail breached, and ticket volume per team fell measurably for AI teams.

Evidence grades

  • A: randomized holdout or randomized wave order, registered before go-live, diagnostics pass.
  • B: staggered rollout or comparison group with pre-trend and placebo tests passed.
  • C: valid but weaker (e.g. fewer than 12 teams; synthetic control and interrupted time series from v2): counts, flagged as weaker.
  • D: diagnostics failed: indicative only, never added to totals.
  • E: vendor estimates, surveys and self-reported time: shown as claimed, never added.

Data handling

  • Person and record identifiers are replaced at ingest with keyed HMAC-SHA256 pseudonyms (requester_id, assignee_id, unit_id).
  • Free text is used only for work typing and dropped before analysis.
  • Teams with fewer people than the minimum group size are left out; results are team-level only.
  • Trailing weeks with too many unresolved records are dropped (right-censoring guard).
  • Exclusions: missing team_id: 74; duplicate ID: 37; resolved before opened: 106

Pilot requests

When you ask for a pilot, we collect the name, work email, company, role and message you enter. We use them only to reply to you and to discuss a pilot. We keep them for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Requests are collected through a form provider linked from the pilot page, which processes them on our behalf.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Customer data in the product

Data that customers process in Probative Labs Cloud is governed by their agreement and data processing terms with us, not by this notice. See Security and privacy.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you sent us. Reply to any message from us, or use the pilot form to reach us.

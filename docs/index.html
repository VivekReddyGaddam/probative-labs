The deflection trap

Your dashboard may say the AI made things worse when it made them better.

When AI absorbs the easy tickets, people are left with harder ones, so average resolution time can rise even though every type of ticket got faster. We compare like with like and show both numbers.

How we measure what changed

The same five steps for every AI program.

Every result gets a range and an evidence grade from A to E. Read the method.

  1. Lock the question

    Metric and quality limits are registered before results exist.

  2. Compare like with like

    Tickets are typed, so a change in the mix never poses as an effect.

  3. Use the right comparison

    Teams without the AI yet show what would have happened anyway.

  4. Check quality

    Faster with more reopens or unhappy users earns nothing.

  5. State the uncertainty

    Ranges, diagnostics and a grade, never one bare number.

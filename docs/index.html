The question nobody checks

“Resolved” includes the people who just stopped answering.

ServiceNow's own guides describe self-solve analytics that mark a conversation resolved when at least one AI asset ran, and a resolution rate that counts no response as positive. A chat the person walked away from closes as "Closed Complete, No User Response".

It isn't only ServiceNow. Intercom bills its Fin agent at $0.99 a resolution and counts one when the customer "exits the conversation without requesting further assistance". It deducts the charge if the customer returns to the same conversation. What about the ones who come back in a new conversation, or by phone?

Nothing in the dashboard checks whether that person came back an hour later. The free check does, with your own two exports.

Your instance, not a generic one

Built for how ServiceNow actually works.

Generic logic would flag your bot for doing its job. The check doesn't.

Incidents the bot opens itself

A topic that ends by opening an incident (Contact type: Virtual Agent) is a handoff, not a failure. Those conversations are left out and counted separately.

Live agent handoffs

A chat a live agent took over isn't the bot's resolution. The check reads the Agent chat field, and won't run without it.

Phone, walk-up and Teams

Anyone who comes back counts when your team logs an incident, whatever the channel. Contacts nobody logs can't be seen, after the bot or after your agents.

Catalog requests

A topic that submits a request isn't a failure, and a request opened after a chat isn't counted as a return.

Your data, checked first

Exports in different time zones, a test account with hundreds of chats, or files that don't share person IDs are flagged before you see a number.

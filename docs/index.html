The question nobody checks

“Resolved” includes the people who just stopped answering.

ServiceNow's own guides describe self-solve analytics that mark a conversation resolved when at least one AI asset ran, and a resolution rate that counts no response as positive. A chat the person walked away from closes as "Closed Complete, No User Response".

Nothing in the dashboard checks whether that person opened an incident an hour later. The free check does, with your own two exports.

  • Each close reason on its own: Topic Complete, No User Response and the rest

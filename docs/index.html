The problem

The same kind of AI tool can help, do nothing, or hurt.

Most AI ROI figures are usage multiplied by assumed minutes saved. Careful studies show why that is not enough: the real effect depends on the task, the people and the organization, and users cannot tell by feel.

The deflection trap

Your dashboard may say the AI made things worse when it made them better.

When AI absorbs the easy tickets, people are left with the harder ones. Average resolution time can rise even though every type of ticket got faster. Probative Labs types every unit of work and compares like with like, then shows both numbers side by side.

How it works

The same five steps for every AI program.

  1. Lock the question

    Hypothesis, metric and quality guardrails are registered before results exist.

  2. Compare like with like

    Every ticket is typed, so a shift in the work mix is never mistaken for an effect.

  3. Compare against the right group

    Teams that did not have the AI yet show what would have happened anyway.

  4. Check quality

    Faster with more reopens or unhappy users earns nothing. A breach blocks the value.

  5. State the uncertainty

    Every number is a range, with diagnostics and an evidence grade.

Evidence grades

Not one blended number. Graded evidence.

Only grades A to C count toward measured ROI. Vendor estimates are shown beside the measured result so leaders can see the gap.

A

Randomized rollout, registered before go-live.

B

Staggered rollout or comparison group, diagnostics passed.

C

Valid but weaker design; counts, flagged.

D

Diagnostics failed; indicative only.

E

Claimed: dashboards, surveys, self-reports.

Capacity is not cash

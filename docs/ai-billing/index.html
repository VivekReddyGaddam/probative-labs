AI billing

However the AI is billed, check what you got.

Per resolution, per conversation, per session, in assists or credits, or per seat: each model hides waste in a different place. Every one is checked against what happened next, and brought to one number you can compare: what each resolution that held cost.

ServiceNow

Now Assist bills in assists. Its reporting counts a walk-away as resolved.

ServiceNow's own guides describe self-solve analytics that mark a conversation resolved when at least one AI asset ran, and a resolution rate that counts no response as positive. A chat the person left without finishing closes as "Closed Complete, No User Response".

The check reads the Interactions table directly and shows, for each way ServiceNow closed a conversation, how often the person needed help again. For assists, it shows where they went by skill, how many ran in development and test instances, how many went to conversations that failed, and when the pool runs out.

Questions

Our contract has no per-resolution price. Is this still useful?

Yes. Whatever the unit, the question at renewal is what the AI delivered for what it cost. Per-conversation, per-session, usage and seat pricing all come down to the same number here, what each resolution that held cost, with a list of what to raise with the vendor.

What do you need from us?

Your ticket export and the AI's conversations, plus the bill or the usage export. For seats, the fee and the number of seats. Everything runs on exports, read-only.

Can we run it ourselves?

Yes: probative reconcile --model, probative usage and probative seats are in the free engine. See what Cloud adds.

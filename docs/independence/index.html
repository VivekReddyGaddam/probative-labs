Independence

Nobody can pay to change the answer.

Probative Labs checks what AI vendors claim. That only works if we are independent of them, so these rules are public and you can hold us to them.

  1. We sell no AI. We do not build, resell or implement AI products, and we take no referral or partner fees from AI vendors.
  2. Vendors cannot pay for results. We do not accept payment from an AI vendor to evaluate, certify or rank its product. A vendor cannot buy, preview, edit or suppress a customer's results or a benchmark figure.
  3. Customers pay per measured program, never a share of the savings found. A share of savings would reward finding value, which is the opposite of neutral measurement.
  4. The method is open. The method and engine are open source. Every result records the engine version, data fingerprints and settings, so anyone with the same data can rerun it and get the same answer. Method changes are published with their validation before they ship.
  5. Results belong to the customer. We never publish a customer's results, or name a customer, without written permission.
  6. Benchmarks are anonymous and aggregate. Contributions contain no names, ticket text or money, and a figure is shown only when at least five organizations have contributed. Contributions are never sold.
  7. Conflicts are disclosed. If anyone working on your analysis has a financial interest in an AI vendor being measured, we tell you before the work starts.

If these rules change, the change is recorded publicly, with its date, before it takes effect.
